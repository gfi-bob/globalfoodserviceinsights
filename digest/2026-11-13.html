November 13, 2026

Global Food & Drink Industry Weekly Digest

Covering foodservice and food & drink industry news from approximately November 2–13, 2026, across North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, Latin America, and the Middle East/Africa.

1. Restaurants & Chains (Global)

  • North America McDonald's reported Q3 global comparable sales growth of just 1.5%, down from 3.6% a year earlier; CEO Chris Kempczinski confirmed on the Nov. 4 earnings call that the chain is testing two new value-menu formats across roughly 1,900 US restaurants in an eight-week trial, targeting a national rollout in early 2027.
  • North America Dine Brands (Applebee's/IHOP) posted Q3 revenue up 15.1% to $248.9 million on an expanding company-owned portfolio, but net income fell 35.7%; the company is leaning into store-level investments including dual-brand conversions and Applebee's remodels.
  • North America BJ's Restaurants notched its ninth straight quarter of growth, with Q3 comparable sales up 8.1% and traffic up 8.3%; the chain raised full-year same-store sales guidance to roughly 4.7%.
  • North America El Pollo Loco's Q3 comparable sales rose 5%, its fourth consecutive quarter of growth, though profit dipped on higher G&A costs; CEO Liz Williams credited menu items like Loaded Quesadillas and continued digital/loyalty gains.
  • North America Multi-brand franchisor Craveworthy Brands agreed to acquire New England pizza chain Papa Gino's (160+ restaurants, ~$160 million in annual revenue) and plans to add its Wing It On! concept inside Papa Gino's locations — its largest acquisition to date, pushing Craveworthy toward roughly $550 million in systemwide sales.
  • North America/Caribbean Encanto Restaurants, the KFC and Taco Bell franchisee in Puerto Rico, filed for bankruptcy days after Yum Brands terminated its franchise relationship; Yum is now searching for a new operator on the island.
  • Europe UK-born Neapolitan pizza chain Pizza Pilgrims opened its first restaurant outside Britain, in Munich, under majority owner L'Osteria (the German pizza-pasta group), with two further Munich openings already lined up as the brand targets 100 UK/Germany sites by 2030.
  • Asia-Pacific China's restaurant count fell 3.3% year-on-year to 7.475 million outlets in October, with average spending per diner down 2.1% to 32.52 yuan, according to data from Hongcan and the Hongcan Research Institute; full-service restaurants were among the few segments still growing (translated from Chinese industry data via Hongcan).
  • Global/Asia-Pacific Technomic data presented at the Global Restaurant Leadership Conference in Cannes projects the global restaurant industry will close 2026 with about 5.2% nominal growth (1.9% real growth), with Asia-Pacific identified as the primary driver of global sales and menu trends.
  • Latin America Brazil's food industry sales into the foodservice channel (restaurants, bars, lanchonetes, bakeries) are projected to exceed R$310 billion in 2026, according to the Brazilian food industry association, underscoring continued growth in the country's out-of-home dining market.

2. Suppliers & Equipment

This section was thin this week relative to the other three; most verifiable, dated news fell under leadership moves rather than new equipment or distribution deals. Searches of Foodservice Equipment & Supplies, FoodBev Media, and general business press did not surface additional dated equipment/distribution stories within the 7–10 day window beyond what's listed below.

  • North America Foodservice equipment maker Middleby secured a new $3 billion five-year multicurrency senior secured credit agreement on Nov. 4, coinciding with a previously announced CEO transition in which COO Steve Spittle will succeed Tim FitzGerald in April 2027.
  • North America GoTo Foods named Brandon Coleman — formerly CEO of Cotton Patch Cafe and earlier CEO of TGI Fridays — as President of McAlister's Deli and the company's deli brands, effective Nov. 2.
  • North America Multi-brand platform Craveworthy Brands hired former McDonald's and Cleveland Avenue finance executive Keith Kravcik as CFO, effective Nov. 2, to help guide its expansion across 20-plus brands.

3. CPG & Beverage

  • North America Buitoni USA (owned by Artisan Chef Manufacturing Company) is closing its 240,000-square-foot pasta factory in Danville, Virginia, around Dec. 28, cutting more than 200 jobs; no reason was given in the WARN notice, and Nestlé — which sold the North American pasta brand in 2020 — still owns Buitoni's pizza business in Europe.
  • North America Niagara Bottling is laying off 85 employees tied to its Ocala, Florida plant as the bottled-water maker scales back production and volume, according to a WARN notice filed in early November.
  • Middle East/Africa UAE-based Agthia Group reported profit roughly tripling with nine-month revenue reaching $1.1 billion, reported Nov. 4.
  • Middle East/Africa Saudi dairy and food giant Almarai announced plans to invest more than $4 billion through 2031 to expand production capacity, enter new categories, and deepen its presence in priority markets, disclosed Nov. 4.

4. Regulatory/Watch

  • North America The 2026 US midterm election results are being watched closely by restaurant brands for their effect on federal policy covering immigration reform, credit-card swipe fees, and USMCA/trade policy — all top priorities the National Restaurant Association has pushed Congress to address, with analysts noting the lame-duck session is now franchisors' and franchisees' most immediate point of leverage.
  • Europe The European Commission issued Recommendation (EU) 2026/2335 on Nov. 3, calling on member states and food businesses to monitor chlorinated paraffins in food through 2029.
  • Europe The EU expanded, via Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/908 (effective Nov. 5), its list of shelf-stable composite products exempt from official controls at border posts, and separately amended import requirements for bivalve molluscs, echinoderms, tunicates, and marine gastropods under Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/1304.
  • Europe Also effective in the Nov. 4–8 window: the EU updated its public-health list of third countries (including Albania, Argentina, Egypt, Kenya, Morocco and others) allowed to export specified animal products to the EU, and approved curdlan (E 424) as a food additive for use across a wide range of food products.

A note on coverage: Despite active searches of non-English trade and business press (including Lebensmittel Zeitung and Tageskarte for Germany, LSA and L'Hôtellerie-Restauration for France, FIPE-Confcommercio-linked coverage for Italy, Alimarket for Spain, Mercado&Consumo for Brazil, and Nikkei-adjacent food trade press for Japan), most matching items were either outside the 7–10 day window or were general market commentary rather than discrete, dated news events. The China restaurant-count item above is one of the few non-English-sourced data points that cleared the recency bar this week.
