November 6, 2026

Global Food & Drink Industry Weekly Digest

Covering roughly October 30 – November 6, 2026, with a handful of highly significant items from October 27–29 included because of their weight. Web search budget for this run was exhausted before a few promising leads (notably on tariffs/trade policy) could be date-verified; those are flagged or omitted rather than risk stale or unconfirmed claims. Items sourced from non-English press are explicitly marked "(translated from ...)."

1. Restaurants & Chains (Global)

North America

  • McDonald's (USA) reported Q3 2026 results (Nov 4): global comparable sales up 1.5%, but U.S. comps slipped -0.2% as higher average checks offset weaker traffic; EPS rose 10% to $3.51 on revenue of ~$7.16B (a slight miss), and the dividend was raised 4%. CNBC
  • Wendy's (USA) beat Q3 2026 earnings estimates on the bottom line, but same-store sales fell 7.8%, underscoring continued struggles in its "brand revitalization" turnaround (reported Nov 5). Rallies.ai
  • Starbucks (USA) closed fiscal Q4/FY2026 (reported Oct 29) with North America comp sales up 7.9% — its first sustained U.S. growth streak under CEO Brian Niccol's turnaround — and raised FY2026 guidance. FastCasual
  • Chipotle (USA) posted Q3 2026 results (Oct 28) showing net income down 11% YoY to $339.9M; shares suffered one of their worst single-day drops in over a decade after the company cut its comparable-sales outlook to flat. Capital.com
  • Brinker International/Chili's (USA) reported Q1 FY2027 results (Oct 28) with Chili's comps up 10.1% and total sales of $1.46B, prompting raised full-year guidance. Kalkine
  • Dine Brands/Applebee's & IHOP (USA) reported Q3 2026 earnings (Nov 4); coverage of adjusted EPS vs. consensus is inconsistent across outlets, so treat specific figures as provisional pending the primary release. TipRanks
  • Cracker Barrel (USA): Sardar Biglari's seventh proxy contest against the board is intensifying ahead of the Nov. 19 annual meeting, with proxy advisory firms ISS, Glass Lewis and Egan-Jones reportedly backing Biglari's challenge to incumbent directors. Nation's Restaurant News

Asia-Pacific

  • Yum China (KFC/Pizza Hut China) reported Q3 2026 results (Nov 3): revenue up 8% YoY to $3.5B, operating profit up 13% to $451M, a record 692 net new stores (19,989 total), and a raised 2026 net-new-store target of 2,400+; the quarter included its completed acquisition of the Pizza Hut Mainland China brand. PR Newswire via Finviz

Europe

  • exki (Belgium), a fast-casual chain, posted its first profit in six years. RetailDetail EU
  • Alsea Europa (Spain), a restaurant-franchise operator, reported sales up 4% to over €293M through June — "(translated from Alimarket, Spain)". Alimarket
  • An investor group led by Nazca Capital acquired restaurant chain Taberna del Volapié and the retail-restaurant business of bakery group Europastry — "(translated from Alimarket, Spain)". Alimarket

Latin America

  • Grupo Nutresa (Colombia) closed its acquisition of the 45-year-old Presto burger chain's assets out of bankruptcy (signed Oct 22, closed Nov 5); combined with its existing El Corral chain, Nutresa now operates 390 restaurants, with Presto reopening ~98 locations and creating 100+ jobs. La República (translated from La República, Colombia)

Middle East/Africa

  • Coverage was genuinely thin this week. The one lead found — Americana Restaurants' (Gulf QSR operator) Q3 2026 results — carried conflicting profit figures across two outlets (one reporting a 63% jump, another a 54% decline) that could not be reconciled before this run's search budget ran out, so it is omitted here pending verification rather than risk publishing a wrong number.

2. Suppliers & Equipment

North America

  • Sysco reported Q1 FY2027 results (Nov 3): sales up 2.8% to $21.7B, adjusted EPS up 3.5% to $1.19; it trimmed full-year sales-growth guidance to the low end of its 6–7% range citing lower inflation, and disclosed a $234M gain tied to its pending Jetro Restaurant Depot acquisition. SEC 8-K
  • US Foods reported Q3 2026 results (Nov 5): net sales of $10.4B (+2.1%), net income up 42.5% to $218M; it tightened FY26 EBITDA guidance downward on a "sustained slower macroeconomic environment" and higher fuel costs, and disclosed it closed its acquisition of Prime Line Distributors shortly after quarter-end.
  • Middleby Corporation posted record Q3 2026 net sales of $652.6M (+7.7% organic, reported Nov 5) and raised full-year guidance; the company is now positioning itself as a "pure-play commercial foodservice" business after spinning off its Food Processing unit (now independent as Midera) and is closing its Brewing equipment line by year-end. It also announced a CEO transition — Steve Spittle will succeed Tim FitzGerald effective April 1, 2027.
  • A monthly foodservice-packaging M&A roundup published this week (Nov 2) flagged several October deals: AmerCareRoyal acquired molded-fiber foodservice packaging maker Ultra Green Packaging; Central National Gottesman is acquiring paper/packaging distributor Omaha Paper Co.; and Millcraft reportedly acquired Kentucky-based Protective Packaging Products (P3). Packaging Dive

Latin America

  • Brazilian manufacturer Tramontina is expanding its professional kitchen-equipment line targeting Brazil's roughly 1.2 million food-away-from-home businesses, aiming to quadruple that unit's revenue — "(translated from Mercado&Consumo, Brazil)".

International (Europe/Asia-Pacific) supplier and equipment-maker news was notably thin this week; prior leads on Ali Group, Electrolux, DKSH and Rational AG all dated to earlier in 2026 and were excluded as stale.

3. CPG & Beverage

Europe

  • AB InBev reported Q3 2026 results (Oct 30): underlying EPS up 22.4% YoY, beating estimates. Vinetur/Reuters
  • Carlsberg reported Q3 2026 results (~Oct 29–30): group revenue rose despite a 3.5% fall in beer volumes, driven by weak China demand; FY2026 guidance was maintained. RTTNews/Grocery Gazette
  • Heineken posted a Q3 2026 trading update (Oct 28) with the Heineken brand's volume up 9.7% and organic net revenue up 4.6%.
  • Mondelez reported a European sales decline alongside Q3 results — "(translated from Lebensmittelpraxis, Germany)".
  • Danone reported Q3 2026 sales growth (~4% cited) and announced COO Vikram Agarwal will retire — a leadership change layered onto the earnings news.
  • Diageo: trade press (Drinks Intel, flagged in The Grocer's weekly roundup) reported three more senior executives are set to depart as part of new CEO Dave Lewis's restructuring; exact date unconfirmed but coverage suggests this is current.

Middle East

  • Almarai (Saudi Arabia), the region's largest dairy company, reported Q3 2026 profit up roughly 13% YoY. The National

North America

  • Kraft Heinz reported Q3 2026 results (Nov 4) alongside continued updates on its planned split from the Oscar Mayer grocery business, amid softer North American demand.
  • Coca-Cola beat Q3 2026 estimates on volume strength (Oct 27) and raised full-year guidance.

Asia-Pacific

  • Nestlé's Malaysia unit: analyst commentary ahead of parent-company results flagged that year-end festive demand is expected to lift Q4 profit. The Star
  • Eastroc Beverage (China), maker of the "Dongpeng Special Drink" energy-drink brand, was spotlighted for pursuing overseas/global expansion — "(translated from Yicai Global, China)"; full article text could not be retrieved, so treat as a headline-level lead pending verification.

Latin America

  • Coca-Cola FEMSA's Brazilian unit announced a R$75M investment in a new distribution center in Limeira, São Paulo state (Nov 5) — "(translated from Mercado&Consumo, Brazil)".

4. Regulatory/Watch Items

Europe

  • The UK's Autumn Budget (delivered ~Oct 29) drew sharp criticism from food, drink and hospitality trade bodies for offering little cost relief to restaurants and F&B manufacturers, with inflation pressure expected to persist into 2027. Food Manufacture
  • A wave of IoT-based cyberattacks disabled supermarket freezers (forcing them into defrost mode, with temperatures reaching 50°C) at roughly 700 sites in France and additional sites across 12 other European countries this week — a cold-chain security incident with direct foodservice supply-chain implications (see related Thailand item below). The Nation, Thailand

Asia-Pacific

  • Thailand's Department of International Trade Promotion (DITP) is assessing export risk to Thai frozen-seafood and processed-chicken exporters following the European supermarket-freezer cyberattacks noted above; no order cancellations have been reported yet, but DITP is urging cold-chain cybersecurity upgrades. Thai exports to the EU hit $24.1B for Jan–Sept 2026, up 23.4% YoY. The Nation, Thailand (Nov 6)

Latin America

  • Brazil's restaurant association Abrasel warned that a proposed 11.3% CBS consumption-tax rate on foodservice, part of Brazil's broader tax reform, would raise costs for restaurants — "(translated from Mercado&Consumo, Brazil)" (Nov 2).
  • A Brazilian senator proposed an amendment that would have the Central Bank regulate meal- and food-voucher card operators, a payment mechanism restaurants rely on heavily — "(translated from Mercado&Consumo, Brazil)" (Nov 2).

North America

  • Routine U.S. food-safety tracking: a recall/public-health-alert roundup covering Nov 3–4, and a reported death under investigation for suspected wild-mushroom poisoning (exact date unconfirmed, early November). eFoodAlert / Food Safety News

Omitted pending verification: Several reported U.S. trade/tariff developments — a rollback of tariffs on ~200 food and beverage imports (reportedly including cocoa, coffee and beef), new tariffs on roughly 60 economies tied to forced-labor compliance rules, and a reported U.S. import ban on Canadian alcohol and dairy — surfaced in this week's searches but their dates could not be confirmed as falling within the past 7–10 days before this run's search budget was exhausted. They are left out here rather than risk including stale or miscategorized news; worth a dedicated follow-up if trade policy is a priority area.
