November 20, 2026

Global Food & Drink Industry Weekly Digest

Covering global foodservice, supplier, CPG/beverage, and regulatory news from approximately November 10–20, 2026.

1. Restaurants & Chains (Global)

  • North America Papa John's confirmed it received a take-private acquisition proposal from TriArtisan Capital Advisors at roughly $65 per share (about $2.7 billion including debt), with the board reviewing the offer as of November 10–12. Source: Reuters/CNBC.
  • North America Bankruptcy-related distress continued to ripple through casual and family dining: Portillo's disclosed additional restaurant closures and an Outback Steakhouse-brand franchisee filed for Chapter 11 protection, both reported November 11, underscoring continued pressure on mid-tier U.S. chains. Source: Restaurant Business/NRN.
  • North America A notable wave of CMO and marketing-chief turnover hit at least six major U.S. chains in the past week (reported November 16), as brands reset leadership heading into 2027 planning season. Source: Restaurant Dive/Marketing Dive.
  • Asia-Pacific Technomic's Ignite global chain-restaurant data, presented at the Global Restaurant Leadership Conference (GRLC) in Cannes, projected 5.2% nominal (1.9% real) global chain sales growth for 2026, with Asia-Pacific leading at roughly 4% real growth. China-based chains stood out, with systemwide sales up about 19.1%, led by Luckin Coffee and Mixue. Reported November 10. Source: Technomic/Nation's Restaurant News.
  • Latin America Arcos Dorados (McDonald's largest franchisee in Latin America and the Caribbean) reported Q3 revenue of roughly $1.3 billion, up 11.3% year-over-year, reported November 12. Source: Company earnings release/Reuters.
  • Latin America Alsea, the master franchisee for several global chains in Latin America, agreed to sell its 107-unit Burger King Argentina business, reported November 13, as the company continues to reshape its regional brand portfolio. Source: Reuters/Valor Econômico.
  • Middle East/Africa Americana Restaurants (operator of KFC, Pizza Hut, and other brands across the Middle East and North Africa) reported nine-month revenue of about $2.1 billion and disclosed a roughly 30% stake sale/IPO process running November 14–22. Reported November 14. Source: Reuters/company disclosures.

2. Suppliers & Equipment

No new supplier or foodservice-equipment news this week.

3. CPG & Beverage

  • North America Mars Inc. announced a $1.4 billion U.S. manufacturing investment planned for 2027, aimed at expanding domestic production capacity. Reported November 13. Source: Food Industry Executive.
  • North America General Mills announced a reorganization into three operating segments, effective January 1, 2027, as part of a broader portfolio and efficiency push. Reported November 13. Source: Food Industry Executive.
  • North America Intersnack completed a stockholder vote finalizing its approximately $2.9 billion take-private acquisition of Utz Brands, with the vote completing November 13. Source: Food Industry Executive/company disclosure.
  • North America Hain Celestial was reported to be weighing a potential Chapter 11 filing amid continued financial strain, reported November 13. Source: Food Industry Executive.
  • North America Store-brand (private label) grocery penetration reached a record 23.7% unit share, according to data cited in the same week's trade coverage, reported November 13. Source: Food Industry Executive.

4. Regulatory/Watch

  • North America The FDA finalized revocation of authorization for three synthetic color-additive solvents — methylene chloride, ethylene dichloride, and trichloroethylene — effective January 11, 2027, continuing the agency's push on food color-additive safety. Reported November 13. Source: Food Industry Executive/FDA.
  • North America Congressional Democrats formed five affordability-focused working groups ahead of the start of the 120th Congress on January 3, 2027, signaling food and grocery costs will remain a live legislative issue. Reported November 13. Source: Food Industry Executive.
  • Global New geopolitical-risk data cited this week found that 81% of consumers (per Euromonitor) and 37% of businesses (per BSI) report feeling the impact of global conflict and trade disruption on food costs and supply chains. Reported November 13. Source: Food Industry Executive.
