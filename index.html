Bob's Take · Week of November 13, 2026

Popcorn on the Burger, Discounts on the Side

By Bob O'Brien

The biggest restaurant chain in the world grew its global comparable sales by…wait for it…1.5 percent last quarter, down from 3.6 percent a year ago. McDonald's answer is the one this industry always reaches for when traffic goes soft: value. It's testing two new value-menu formats in roughly 1,900 U.S. restaurants ahead of a national rollout early next year, and in the meantime there's an $8 Quarter Pounder meal for anyone who has priced a combo lately (I have, and I winced). Nobody is immune. China, which posted double-digit traffic gains quarter after quarter not so long ago, now has 3.3 percent fewer restaurants than a year ago, and the average diner there is spending 2.1 percent less. Technomic has the global industry finishing 2026 up about 5 percent, which shrinks to 1.9 percent once you take inflation out. Growth, yes. Exciting, no.

So naturally the marketing departments have turned it up to eleven. KFC has signed on with Marvel's "Avengers: Doomsday" in more than 100 countries (Doctor Doom packaging, for those who like a little menace with their bucket). Burger King in Spain and Portugal is putting popcorn on a bacon burger. Domino's in Singapore has a pizza with a burger for a crust, and Starbucks is pouring something called Tiramisu Velvet ahead of Red Cup Day. I'm all for fun, but spectacle gets you a visit and value and execution get you the next one. BJ's Restaurants grew traffic 8.3 percent this quarter, its ninth straight quarter of growth, and as far as I can tell it did it without a single superhero.
