Newsletter · November 13, 2026

Global Chain Marketing Weekly

Covering marketing campaigns, promotions, LTOs, new product launches and brand collaborations from approximately November 2–13, 2026, worldwide, for McDonald's, Burger King/Hungry Jack's, Subway, KFC, Pizza Hut, Domino's, Starbucks, and Costa Coffee.

McDonald's

  • (US) The 2026 holiday menu launched Nov. 3, featuring a Frosted Cranberry Splash (made with Sprite), two Red Bull Frosted Cranberry Energizer variants, and two Cookie Butter Frappés — plus an $8 Quarter Pounder with Cheese meal deal (QPC, medium fries, medium drink) that started Nov. 2 and is priced well below the typical $11+ combo cost. Source: McDonald's USA newsroom, Nation's Restaurant News.
  • (UK/Ireland) "World Menu Heist 2.0" is running Sept. 22–Nov. 16, bringing international items into UK/Ireland restaurants: the Big Rösti (from Germany), Chicken & Egg McMuffin and Chicken & Bacon McMuffin (from Australia), Mac & Cheese Triangles (from the UAE), and McShaker Fries. Source: McDonald's UK newsroom, The Drum.
  • (Germany) The "Endlich wieder bei Mecces" ("Finally back at Mecces") campaign is running Oct. 28–Nov. 24, centered on the 1955 Burger paired with branded retro radios as a promotional giveaway item. (translated from W&V, Germany)
  • (Japan) A new "Cheese Cheese Teriyaki Chicken Juicy Mac" (double-cheese variant of the Teriyaki Chicken Juicy Mac) launched Nov. 4–24; separately, a Nov. 18–Dec. 15 campaign discounts the 15-piece Chicken McNugget box to ¥490 (250 yen off) with two new dipping sauces — a Sukiyaki-style sauce and a truffle cheese sauce — alongside a San-X Universe (Rilakkuma/Sumikko Gurashi) collectible packaging tie-in and a TV spot starring actor Masato Sakai as "Mr. Tokuninaldo." (translated from Nikkei/Shokuhin Sangyo Shimbun, Japan)

Burger King / Hungry Jack's

  • (Spain/Portugal) Burger King launched the "Bacon King Pop" on Nov. 3 — a Bacon King burger topped with, and served alongside, popcorn — exclusive to Spain and Portugal; CMO Yvette Altet (Burger King Spain) commented on the launch. (translated from Alimarket, Spain)
  • (India) Tied to the Marvel "Avengers: Doomsday" film tie-in window (around Nov. 4), Burger King India introduced a black-bunned Whopper covered in a green spicy garlic sauce as a regional promotional item. Source: Marketing-Interactive, India trade press.
  • (UK) A Steakhouse Crispy Chicken burger promotion offers buy-one-get-one-free via the BK app starting Nov. 18, expanding to wider in-restaurant and delivery availability from Nov. 22. Source: Campaign, Little Black Book.
  • (US) Burger King's 2026 Advent Calendar — a build-your-own mini BK restaurant playset — went on sale at $19.54, with daily drops of 110 calendars Nov. 16–20 exclusively via BKAdventCalendar.com. Source: Marketing Dive, QSR Magazine.

Subway

  • (US) A buy-one-get-one-free Footlong promotion for Sub Club loyalty members ran Oct. 27–Nov. 8, notable for bringing back the Fritos Chicken Enchilada Sub after a 12-year absence from the menu. Source: Subway newsroom, QSR Magazine.
  • (US) National Sandwich Day celebrations were moved up to Oct. 27 in 2026 (ahead of the usual Nov. 3 date) to avoid falling on US midterm election day. Source: Marketing Dive.

KFC

  • (Global — 100+ countries) KFC launched a global collaboration with Marvel Studios' "Avengers: Doomsday" on Nov. 9, running through January 2027 across more than 100 participating countries. The tie-in includes limited-time menu items and drinks, Doctor Doom-themed packaging, and exclusive Marvel-inspired KFC collectible figurines. Source: KFC global newsroom, The Drum.

Pizza Hut

  • (UK) A Christmas/festive pizza menu introduced three new recipes — Brie, Bacon & Cranberry; Brie & Cranberry; and a loaded "Festive Feast" pizza — each available with a 50%-off promotional offer; CMO Jana Ulaite commented on the launch. Source: Pizza Hut UK newsroom, Campaign.

Domino's

  • (Singapore, expanding to Cambodia) A burger-pizza fusion campaign — "Domino's Kembara World Cheese Tour" — introduced an all-new burger-crust pizza paired with loaded cheeseburger crispy fries; it rolled out in Singapore on Nov. 5, with a Cambodia launch to follow, created with agency FCB Shout. Source: Marketing-Interactive, Campaign Asia.
  • (Global) A Minecraft Dungeons II collaboration campaign (first reported Oct. 21) appears to still be active into the Nov. 2–13 window based on continued promotional activity, though the core announcement itself falls just outside the strict 7–10 day cutoff and is noted here for context rather than as a fresh item.

Starbucks

  • (US) The 2026 winter holiday menu launched Nov. 5, headlined by a new Tiramisu Velvet drink line, an Apple Pomegranate Refresher, and a Snowy Apple Drink made with coconutmilk; new holiday food items include a Braised Short Rib Grilled Cheese and a Turkey & Stuffing Sandwich. Red Cup Day is set for Nov. 17, offering a free reusable cup with a qualifying holiday beverage purchase. Separately, mystery "Bearista Glass" cold cups began selling in surprise boxes on Nov. 10. Source: Starbucks newsroom, Marketing Dive, Nation's Restaurant News.
  • (Japan) A "Let's par-TEA!" holiday tea beverage line launched Nov. 2, including a Joyful Medley Strawberry Creamy Tea Latte, an Earl Grey Chocolate Frappuccino, and a Royal Milk Tea, accompanied by a Santa-hat Bearista glass tumbler (¥4,600). (translated from Impress Watch, Japan)

Costa Coffee

  • (UK/Ireland) The Christmas 2026 menu launched Oct. 22, featuring a Golden Hazel Latte (built around Ferrero Rocher-style flavors) and an Iced Penguin Gingerbread Biscuit launching Nov. 5, plus five new bakery items total alongside returning favorites (All-Butter Mince Pie, Gluten Free Mince Tart, Terry's Chocolate Orange Muffin, Festive Spice Muffin) and new seasonal Mystery Box Plushie merchandise.
  • A Gingerbread Latte became available in the air on Jet2 flights starting Nov. 9 and on Aer Lingus flights starting Nov. 5, extending the holiday range into airline partnerships. Source: Costa Coffee newsroom, The Grocer.
