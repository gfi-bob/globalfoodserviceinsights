Global Chain Marketing Weekly

November 20, 2026

Global Chain Marketing, Promotion & New Product Weekly

Covering marketing campaigns, promotions, LTOs and product launches from approximately November 10–20, 2026, for McDonald's, Burger King/Hungry Jack's, Subway, KFC, Pizza Hut, Domino's, Starbucks and Costa Coffee.

McDonald's

  • United States McDonald's launched a "Home Alone Meal," letting guests turn any order into a themed meal with new items — a Little Nero's Pizza McShaker Fries, a Lovely Cheese Pizza dipping sauce, and an Everything Sundae McFlurry — plus one of four collectible Home Alone-themed socks (one featuring Kevin McCallister alongside Hamburglar, Grimace, and Birdie). Macaulay Culkin reprises Kevin in the campaign's advertising, and the promotion includes a 14-day Disney+/Hulu trial offer and a ticketed fan event in Los Angeles on December 4–5. Reported November 17. Source: Nation's Restaurant News.

Burger King

  • United States Burger King is running its third annual $19.54-priced Advent calendar (a nod to its founding year), sold only at BKAdventCalendar.com in daily drops of 110 units — a total of 550 — from Monday, November 16 through Friday, November 20. Each calendar's 12 doors reveal miniature collectibles (a mini Whopper, nuggets, a soda fountain, a King's Booth, and holiday décor) that assemble into a mini BK restaurant, with the final door containing a $5 Royal Perks digital coupon. Announced November 5–6; the daily sell-out window falls squarely in this week. Source: Dexerto.

Hungry Jack's (Australia — Burger King brand)

No new Hungry Jack's campaigns this week.

Subway

No new Subway campaigns this week.

KFC

  • United States KFC is running its "Finger Lickin' Fan Fest" from November 11–30 at participating U.S. restaurants, giving KFC Rewards members double points on app and KFC.com purchases and expanding the chain's "Secret Recipe Vault" redemption catalog from about a dozen items to 24, spanning 250-, 500-, 750-, and 1,000-point tiers. Members must unlock the offer before purchase, are capped at three redemptions a day, and delivery orders are excluded. Source: QSR Magazine.

Pizza Hut

  • United States Pizza Hut brought back its Triple Treat Box for its 11th holiday season, priced at $21.99 (about $5.50 per person) and running November 16 through December 28. The box includes two medium one-topping pizzas, breadsticks, and cinnamon sticks in festive packaging — a format tweak from 2025's $19.99 version, which bundled five breadsticks and a choice of dessert. CMO Melissa Friebe framed it as a way to simplify a busy holiday dinner decision. Reported November 17. Source: Nation's Restaurant News.

Domino's

  • United States Domino's added a new Sweet Heat Smokehouse specialty pizza — honey barbecue sauce, Italian sausage, smoked bacon, jalapeños, pineapple, onions, provolone, and mozzarella — available nationwide for a limited time as part of its Mix & Match deal, with medium specialty pizzas priced at $9.99 each when bundled with at least one other qualifying item. Reported November 16. Source: Nation's Restaurant News.

Starbucks

  • United States Starbucks' 2026 holiday menu, which returned November 5, is in full swing this week: new drinks include a Tiramisu Velvet Latte and Cold Brew, an Apple Pomegranate Refresher, a coconut-milk Snowy Apple Drink, an Iced Sugar Cookie Cream Matcha, and an Iced Caramel Brulée Cream Chai, alongside new food (a Braised Short Rib Grilled Cheese, a refreshed Turkey & Stuffing Sandwich on cranberry-dough baguette) and a limited-run, company-store-only "Pup Cup" featuring a beagle named Junie. Red Cup Day — when any handcrafted holiday beverage purchase earns a reusable red cup — landed November 17, squarely in this week's window. Source: Nation's Restaurant News.

Costa Coffee

  • UK/Ireland Costa's 2026 Christmas menu, launched Oct. 22, includes a Golden Hazel Latte with Ferrero Rocher, a Gingerbread Iced Matcha Latte and an Ultimate Yule Brownie Hot Chocolate/Latte alongside returning favorites. Tie-ins rolled out Nov. 5–9: an Iced Penguin Gingerbread Biscuit and airline partnerships with Aer Lingus and Jet2.
