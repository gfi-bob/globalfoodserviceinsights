Global Chain Marketing Weekly

November 12, 2026

Global Chain Marketing, Promotion & New Product Weekly

Covering roughly November 2–12, 2026, for McDonald's, Burger King/Hungry Jack's, Subway, KFC, Pizza Hut, Domino's, Starbucks, and Costa Coffee. Sourced from brand newsrooms, marketing trade press, and local news outlets worldwide, in English and other languages (translated and tagged below). Where coverage for a chain or region was genuinely thin this week, that's noted explicitly rather than padded with stale campaigns.

McDonald's

  • United States — McDonald's USA chief marketing & customer experience officer Alyssa Buetikofer is departing (through Dec 18), a day after Q3 U.S. comp sales fell 0.2%; global CMO Morgan Flatley becomes interim chief creative officer and Charlie Newberger steps in as interim U.S. CMO. The piece also notes McDonald's recently stepped away from its 32-year FIFA World Cup sponsorship. (Nov 5) Marketing Dive
  • United States — Rolled out five new holiday drinks nationwide: a Frosted Cranberry Splash with Sprite, regular/Zero Red Bull Frosted Cranberry Energizers, and three Cookie Butter coffee drinks (Frappé, Latte, Iced Coffee). (Nov 2–3) Nation's Restaurant News
  • Germany — "Endlich wieder bei Mecces" nostalgia relaunch brings back the 1955® Burger and McFarmer after a decade of fan requests, plus retro Happy Meal radios loaded with 2000s/2010s pop hits and app-only €1 "Flashback Deals," running through Nov 24. (translated from McDonald's Germany Newsroom) (launched Oct 28, live through window)
  • UK & Ireland — "World Menu Heist 2.0" continues importing international favorites — Germany's Big Rösti, Canada's Maple & Cheddar Chicken, China's Szechuan Sauce, India's Guava x Sprite Zero, and Australia's Chicken & Egg McMuffin among them — with several items running through Nov 16. McDonald's UK Newsroom
  • Taiwan — New "Avocado Sauce Manfu Series" breakfast items launched Nov 4 alongside a Korean-design "Shake Guy Cloud Bag" merch drop and a three-week app-anniversary promotion with daily prize games and LINE buy-3-get-3 coffee deals. (translated from McDonald's Taiwan Newsroom) (Nov 2–4)
  • Japan — Two campaigns running in parallel: a 55th-anniversary fries promotion ("Tokuni Nardo," medium/large fries for ¥250, Nov 2–13) fronted by actors Yuno Nagao and Masato Sakai with a potato-plush rewards tie-in; and a new "Cheese Cheese" burger lineup (Nov 4–24) backed by a TV spot featuring a 100-member fictional tribute band to rock group LUNA SEA. (translated from Impress Watch/Gourmet Watch, Japan)
  • Spain — Launched "MyMcDonald's Big Days," a 29-day rotating app-exclusive deals platform with a grand-prize sweepstakes (trips to the Great Wall, Grand Canyon, or Great Barrier Reef) and double loyalty points on mobile orders. (translated from Marketing Directo, Spain) (Nov 5)

Burger King / Hungry Jack's

  • Spain — Launched the "Bacon King Pop," a bacon-and-cheese burger topped with an exclusive "Bacon Pop" sauce and served with a side of popcorn customers can add directly to the burger. (translated from L'Express Franchise, Spain) (Nov 3–4) — a U.S. outlet reported the LTO as covering Portugal too, but that could not be confirmed via local Portuguese press this week.
  • India — Named official restaurant partner of Avengers: Doomsday (opens Dec 17): a nationwide "Doomsday Whopper" menu from ₹199 includes themed burgers (Loki Molten Cheese, Cyclops Soft Paneer, Magneto Crunchy Chicken), Doctor Doom-inspired packaging, and a full digital/OOH/influencer push. (Nov 3) Media Infoline
  • Brazil — New premium "King Clássico"/"King Duplo Clássico" burgers launched under its "The Kings" platform, backed by an AlmapBBDO ad pairing classical music with burger-assembly visuals. (translated from Meio & Mensagem, Brazil) (Nov 4)
  • United States — Unveiled its third annual limited-edition $19.54 "Build-Your-Own-Restaurant" Advent Calendar (12 mini collectibles assembling into a mini BK restaurant), sold only online with ~110 units released daily at noon ET, Nov 16–20. (Nov 6) Dexerto
  • Coverage note: Hungry Jack's (Australia), UK, France, and Italy had no Burger King marketing news confirmed as dated within this week's window — recent-looking leads for those markets all turned out to be from earlier in 2026 or 2025.

Subway

  • Canada — Launched a new limited-time Holiday Herb Bread (onion, sage, lemon, rosemary and thyme, described as tasting "like stuffing") and partnered with Toronto designer Dorian Who to turn it into a wearable "Holiday Bread Coat" with a realistic bread print and a built-in "Footlong pocket," given away via an Instagram contest running through Nov 23. Also launched an $8.99 Festive Meal of the Day (a different daily 6-inch sub plus Turtles® Caramel Pecan Cookies). (Nov 9) Subway Newsroom
  • UK/EMEA — Expanded its UK development deal with supermarket chain Asda to add up to 70 new in-store restaurants over four years, part of a wider plan to double EMEA store count to 10,000 by 2031; starting January 2027, new/remodeled EMEA stores will adopt a "Blueprint" format adding breakfast/coffee and an evening "Subway After Dark" menu. (Nov 3) Restaurant Online
  • UK & Ireland — Launched a 2026 Christmas menu (Nov 4): an Ultimate Festive Sub (rotisserie chicken, bacon, stuffing, cranberry sauce, and a new "Gravy Mayo") and an Ultimate Festive Spud on sage-and-onion stuffing-topped bread, served with a Gravy Mayo dunk pot, plus two new cookies (Chocolate Hazelnut and Chocolate Filled). Side promos include a collectible weekly "family plushie" giveaway tied to festive Footlong meal deals running Nov 18–Dec 31. Retail Times
  • United States — Moved its National Sandwich Day promotion to Oct 27 (to avoid clashing with the U.S. midterm elections) and brought back the Fritos Chicken Enchilada Sub after a 12-year absence, with a BOGO footlong offer running through Nov 8. Chowhound
  • Coverage note: No confirmed new campaigns this week for India, Australia, Germany, or France.

KFC

  • Global (100 countries) — Launched its biggest-ever global brand partnership, a "Doomified" menu with Marvel Studios tied to Avengers: Doomsday (Doom Burger, Doom Wings, Doom Bucket, Doom Shake) with Doctor Doom-themed packaging and a 5-figure "Colonel Sanders Cosplay Collection," rolling out across 100 markets from Nov 9 through January — notably excluding the U.S., India, and China. (Announced Nov 4, launched Nov 9) MeatPoultry
  • UK & Ireland — Christmas menu brings back the Gravy Burger rebranded as the "Gravymas Burger" (£7.99: Original Recipe fillet, hash brown, melted cheese, sage & onion seasoning, gravy mayo, and a side gravy pot), alongside a Gravymas Box Meal, Festive Loaded Fries, a Fully Festive Meal, and Festive Double/Triple Buckets, running Nov 9–Jan 3. Some fans were disappointed last year's Stuffing Tower/Stuffing Stacker didn't return. BritBrief
  • Coverage note: Promising-looking KFC campaigns in the U.S., India, and the Gulf (Saudi/UAE, where agency TBWA\RAAD has an active pipeline) all dated outside this week's window on verification, so none are included here.

Pizza Hut

  • China — Expanding its frozen durian dessert — described by Yum China CEO Joey Wat as tasting "like durian ice cream, but it's real durian frozen" — into retail/supermarket channels starting early November, part of a broader push into packaged retail products. (Nov 5) Inside Retail Asia
  • China — Context from Yum China's Q3 results: new in-store add-on formats are scaling fast — KPRO light-meal counters (700+ locations, targeting 1,500), Pizza Hut Burger Bar counters (300+ across 50 cities, projected to generate over 1 billion yuan this year), and KCoffee (4,000+ locations) — alongside smaller-format "Pizza Hut WOW" stores for lower-tier cities. (Nov 5)
  • Singapore — New star-shaped "Chicken Starizo" pizza (chicken chorizo, ranch, jalapeño cheese dip center) launched as part of a festive promo running Nov 12–29 at $18.95 versus the usual $44.95. (Nov 4/12) MissLobang
  • Singapore — New "Cheesy Otah" Melt added to a 1-for-1 Melts deal ($9.95 for two) running Nov 1–15. MissLobang
  • Coverage note: No confirmed new-this-week items for the U.S., India, or the Middle East.

Domino's

  • India — Jubilant FoodWorks' Domino's India entered a new snacking category with "Dominoid," a two-slice cheese snack (not a pizza) in four flavors including Korean Heat and Lebanese Shawarma, priced from ₹119, backed by a TV campaign positioning it for youth snacking occasions beyond pizza. (Nov 4–5) Restaurant India
  • UK — Unveiled its Christmas 2026 menu headlined by a new Bourbon Glazed Pulled Pork Pizza (pulled pork, Peppadew peppers, red onion, and a bourbon glaze), alongside returning favorites The Festive One Pizza and a Hot & Cheesy Dip served with Twisted Doughballs; launches nationwide Nov 16 through Jan 3. (Nov 9) Verge Magazine UK
  • Global (~19 markets) — Its Minecraft Dungeons II tie-in — collectible pizza boxes with purchases of "The Domino" single-serve pizza, plus 20,000 digital in-game codes distributed through Nov 4 — wound down at the start of this window across markets including Australia, Canada, Japan, Germany, Brazil, and Saudi Arabia. (promo ran through Nov 4) Nation's Restaurant News
  • United States — Its annual "St. Jude Thanks and Giving" holiday fundraising campaign, launched just before this window, is running through the season as part of a pledge to raise $300M for St. Jude Children's Research Hospital by 2034.
  • Coverage note: No confirmed new-this-week campaigns found for Australia, Southeast Asia, Spain, or the Middle East.

Starbucks

  • United States/Canada — Full 2026 holiday menu launched: new drinks are a Tiramisu Velvet Latte and Cold Brew, Apple Pomegranate Refresher, Snowy Apple Drink (with coconut milk), Iced Sugar Cookie Cream Matcha, and Iced Caramel Brulée Cream Chai; new food is a Braised Short Rib Grilled Cheese plus a refreshed Turkey & Stuffing Sandwich. Also debuting: a first-ever limited "pup cup" (modeled on a beagle named Junie) and mystery Bearista Glass Cold Cup characters dropping Nov 10, ahead of Red Cup Day on Nov 17. (Nov 4) Nation's Restaurant News
  • UK — Holiday menu launched nationwide Nov 5: new Peppermint Mocha, Peppermint Hot Chocolate, Iced Peppermint White Chocolate Matcha, and Peppermint Mocha Frappuccino, alongside returning Toffee Nut, Gingerbread, and Eggnog drinks. Rewards members got early "First Taste" access from Oct 29, and the launch included the chain's first-ever Holiday Cold Cup alongside the traditional Red Cups. Retail Times
  • Japan — Launched a tea-forward "Holiday Tea Party" lineup (Joyful Medley Strawberry Creamy Tea Latte, Earl Grey Chocolate Frappuccino, Royal Milk Tea) nationwide Nov 2, distinct from the Western coffee-centric holiday push. (translated from Have a Good Holiday, Japan)
  • Japan — Its Reserve Roastery Tokyo flagship launched a "Decorate Your Holiday Season" merch collection (glitter bottles, tumblers, a Teavana-branded tea set) in store Nov 2. (translated from Have a Good Holiday, Japan)
  • United States — New "Uncap, Scan & Win" Rewards sweepstakes offering prizes including "Starbucks for a year" and 50,000 free drinks, running Nov 2–Dec 31. DannyDealGuru
  • United States — New holiday ad "Perfect Harmony," hand-drawn by artist Geoff McFetridge for agency Anomaly, paired with a companion 7-track "Holiday Blend" album released via Interscope/Universal and a $500,000 donation to World Central Kitchen. (Nov 6) DesignRush
  • Coverage note: No confirmed new-this-week campaigns found for China, South Korea, or the Middle East.

Costa Coffee

  • UK & Ireland — Its full festive 2026 menu (launched Oct 22, running through this window) includes new drinks — a Golden Hazel Latte with Ferrero Rocher and a Gingerbread Iced Matcha Latte — plus an Ultimate Yule Brownie range, returning Black Forest and Terry's Chocolate Orange Hot Chocolates, new food including a Pigs in Blankets 'Nduja Mac & Cheese, a Dubai-Style Speculoos Tiffin in the bakery case, and Mystery Box Plushies as merch. The one item specifically launching inside this week's window is a new Iced Penguin Gingerbread Biscuit (Nov 5), with 20p from each sale donated to the Costa Foundation charity. The range spans cafes, 14,000+ Costa Express machines, grocery sachets (Tesco, Morrisons, Iceland, Sainsbury's), and in-flight service on Jet2/Aer Lingus. Retail Times
  • Coverage note: Costa generated notably less global trade press than the other chains this week, as expected for a smaller international footprint. No confirmed new campaigns were found for China, the Middle East, or India within this window — worth revisiting as Black Friday/December activity ramps up.

Methodology note: each chain was researched separately across brand newsrooms, marketing trade press (Marketing Dive, The Drum, Campaign, Nation's Restaurant News, QSR Magazine, Mumbrella, FoodBev Media) and local-language outlets. Several promising leads for India, China, Australia, the Middle East, and Latin America could not be date-confirmed within the Nov 2–12, 2026 window before this run's search budget was exhausted — those gaps reflect research limits this week, not necessarily an absence of activity, and are flagged above rather than filled with older campaigns.
