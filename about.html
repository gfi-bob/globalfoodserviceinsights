About Global Foodservice Insights

Global Foodservice Insights publishes three weekly briefings for people who follow the foodservice business around the world. New issues are posted every Friday.

Global Food & Drink Industry Weekly Digest

The week's most important foodservice and food & drink industry news from North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, Latin America and the Middle East/Africa — restaurants and chains, suppliers and equipment, CPG and beverage, and regulation.

Global Chain Marketing, Promotion & New Product Weekly

Marketing campaigns, promotions, limited-time offers and new products from McDonald's, Burger King/Hungry Jack's, Subway, KFC, Pizza Hut, Domino's, Starbucks and Costa Coffee — in every market, drawing on English and non-English sources.

GLP-1 Weekly

News on the GLP-1 weight-loss drugs and the businesses they touch: drug development and approvals, public use and attitudes, products launched for GLP-1 users, and what companies say about the drugs' impact on their quarterly results. Paired with a usage tracker of how many Americans take them.

How it's put together

Each issue is researched from company announcements, trade press and business news in several languages. Items drawn from non-English sources are marked as translated, and sections where little dated news turned up in the week are flagged rather than padded.
